# Privacy Policy — Oops! That Floor!

_Draft for store submission. Effective on first public release._

**Short version: the game collects nothing.**

- No account, sign-in or email is ever requested.
- No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs are included.
- The game makes no network requests. It works fully offline.
- Your progress and settings are stored only on your device (Android
  SharedPreferences / iOS UserDefaults via Capacitor Preferences). Deleting
  the app or using *Settings → Reset progress* removes them.
- The game requests no device permissions.
- If you choose to share a result, the game opens your device's share
  sheet with a generated image and text; what happens next is up to the app
  you pick. The game itself sends nothing anywhere.
- Children: the game contains no data collection, so it is suitable for all
  ages under COPPA/GDPR-K in this respect.

Contact: _add publisher contact before submission._

## Store disclosures

- **Google Play Data safety:** no data collected; no data shared.
- **Apple App Privacy:** Data Not Collected. Privacy manifest
  (`ios/App/App/PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`) declares no tracking and the
  UserDefaults required-reason API (CA92.1).
