# Playtest Protocol

Goal: learn whether new players understand, enjoy and want to continue —
with evidence, not impressions. **No playtest has been run yet; do not
cite results until sessions are recorded.**

## Participants & setup

- 6–10 people who have never seen the game; mix of casual and platformer players.
- Their own phone if possible (Android and iPhone), landscape, sound on, haptics on.
- Fresh install (Settings → Reset progress if reusing a device).
- Screen recording on; facilitator silent unless the player is stuck > 2 minutes.

## Script (≈ 20 minutes)

1. Hand over the phone on the title screen. Say only: *"Play for as long as you like. Think out loud if you can."*
2. Let them play 1-1 → 2-4 (≈ 10 min) without help.
3. Jump them (Level select) to the flagged levels: 2-7, 4-2, 5-1, 5-4, 5-8.
4. Short interview.

## Measure (per player, per level)

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Time to first intentional movement on 1-1 | video timestamp |
| Understood the controls without the hints? | observe |
| Attempts and time to clear | in-game death counter + video |
| "Aha" moments (laugh, "oh!", "WHAT") | tally from think-aloud |
| Frustration markers (sigh, "this is unfair", quitting) | tally |
| Mis-taps / accidental jumps | observe hands |
| Did they read the surprise correctly afterwards? | ask after each flagged level: "What happened there?" |

## Interview (1–5 scale + why)

1. How quickly did you understand what to do?
2. Were the surprises funny or annoying?
3. When you died, did you know why?
4. Did the controls do what you meant?
5. How much do you want to keep playing?
6. Which moment do you remember most?

## Decision rules

- A level where ≥ 30 % of players can't explain their death → add/clarify a tell.
- Median attempts > 12 on any level before chapter 4 → ease it.
- Any accidental-jump or missed-input report from ≥ 2 players → revisit pad layout.
- Record raw notes in `docs/playtests/YYYY-MM-DD-<initials>.md`.
